👋 Hi, I'm Ying Chen — a bioinformatician at the Vertebrate Genome Lab.
🎓 I obtained my PhD in Biology at Queen's University in Canada.
🧬 I specialize in genome assembly, including high-quality Vertebrate Genome Project genomes and telomere-to-telomere (T2T) genomes.
🌊 My work spans population genomics, comparative genomics, conservation genomics and environmental DNA.
